Privacy and security

  • Core records are stored locally on your device.
  • The app supports PIN protection, biometrics, and encrypted local backups.
  • The free version may display ads; the Pro version removes banners and unlocks extra features.
  • External services are used only when specific features need them.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

The main finance, task, and note features work locally. Some external services require internet access.

Is Financial Peace the same app as MyMoney?

Yes. The app was renamed while keeping the same Android package: com.anspro.mymoney.app.

Features in Financial Peace 1.3.0

Personal finance

Expenses, salary, extra income, installments, subscriptions, recurring bills, search and payment tracking.

Planning and reports

Financial forecasts, charts and monthly insights, calendar, PDF reports and savings tools.

Personal organization

To-do lists, completion percentages, text and voice notes and on-device OCR from selected images.

Pro features

One-time purchase for extra planning features, personal journal and automatic local backups.

Security and privacy

8–16-digit PIN, supported biometrics, encrypted structured records and portable v4 backups.

Notifications and language

Local reminders for bills and tasks, plus optional Firebase announcements in seven languages on compatible versions.

Appearance

Fifteen themes including Classic Green and Dark Theme, with a seven-language interface.

Free and Pro

Essential features are free, with privacy-aware ads; Pro removes banners and enables additional tools.
