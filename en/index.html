BUILT FOR EVERYDAY LIFE

Everything you need, without the hassle

From your monthly budget to task reminders, Financial Peace brings essential tools together to simplify your routine.

Bills and due dates

Track bills, installments, subscriptions, and recurring payments. Check what is paid and what is still due.

Notifications and reminders

Turn on alerts for bills, tasks, notes, and expected income based on your preferences and Android permissions.

Income and available balance

Record salaries and other income, mark payments as received, and see how much money remains available.

Charts and planning

Explore spending categories and view your finances and due dates in a helpful calendar.

Tasks and notes

Create lists, track pending tasks, and organize ideas, scanned documents, and useful information.

Privacy and control

Financial records are stored locally, with optional PIN access. The app does not access or move money in your bank account.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know

Does Financial Peace connect to my bank account?+

No. You enter and manage your own records. The app does not access bank accounts or initiate financial transactions.

Can I use the app offline?+

Core recording and organization features can work offline. Pro purchases or verification, exchange rates, and ads may require internet access.

How do notifications work?+

You choose which reminders to enable and grant Android notification permission. Delivery may vary with battery restrictions, permissions, and device settings.

How is my information protected?+

Main financial records are stored on your device. You can enable a PIN for access, and protected local backups are available in Pro.

Which languages are supported?+

Portuguese, English, and Spanish. You can also choose your country and currency inside the app.
