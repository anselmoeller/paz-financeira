How to organize income, expenses and available balance

Knowing how much you earn is different from knowing how much is available to spend. Upcoming bills and irregular income both matter.

1. Record separate sources of income

Keep salary, freelance work and other income distinct. Mark each amount as received only when it actually arrives.

2. List current and future commitments

Include recurring bills, installments, subscriptions and expected expenses. Remember payments due after the next payday.

3. Compare received, paid and outstanding amounts

Treat received income, paid expenses and unpaid bills as separate figures. Your app records help with planning; they are not a live bank balance.

4. Consider the next month

Review recurring obligations before spending what seems left over today. Forecasts are planning estimates and should be revised when circumstances change.

5. Use a consistent update routine

Update income and payments on the same days each week. Better records produce more useful summaries and charts.

Practical habits to start today

  • Avoid recording the same recurring income twice.
  • Do not mistake projected income for funds received.
  • Review subscriptions and installments before taking on new costs.
