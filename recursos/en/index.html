Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to get you started.

Can I track subscriptions and installments?

Yes. You can record one-time expenses, monthly bills, subscriptions and installment payments.

Does the app include notes and tasks?

Yes. Write notes, scan text, record voice notes and organize task lists.

Can I share PDF reports?

Yes. PDF options are available for finances, personal notes, tasks and income records.

Do I need to pay to use the app?

A free version with ads is available. Pro adds features and removes advertising.

How do local backups work?

You can create and restore protected local backup files. Keep both your file and its protecting PIN safe.
